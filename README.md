## 👨‍💻 About Me

> **"Fikri çalışan bir ürüne çeviriyorum."** — I turn an idea into a product that actually runs.

I'm a **Computer Engineer** who builds mobile products end‑to‑end and brings AI into them where it genuinely solves something. I don't stop at prototypes — my favourite part is taking a feature from requirement gathering all the way to production.

- 🎓 **Graduated** in Computer Engineering from **Kayseri University** with a **3.44 / 4.00 GPA**
- 📱 I ship with **React Native + Expo + TypeScript**, and build cross‑platform apps with **Flutter / Dart**
- 🤖 I work on **deep learning, computer vision and NLP** — and care that a model can *explain* its decision, not just be accurate
- 🏛️ Delivered a **production‑ready municipal app** from scratch during my R&D internship at **Melikgazi Municipality**
- 🔬 **Project lead** of a **TÜBİTAK 2209‑A** funded autonomous reconnaissance vehicle research project
- 🌍 Currently studying **English Prep at Marmara University**
- 👨‍🏫 Volunteer tech instructor — teaching **software, AI and robotics to kids**

## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 🏛️ Çarşı — Municipal Loyalty Platform
> **React Native · Expo · TypeScript · PostgreSQL · REST API**
>
> A municipal app connecting local businesses with citizens. Citizens earn reward points by scanning **QR codes and receipts**; businesses get a **campaign management** panel.
> Built from scratch during my R&D internship at **Melikgazi Municipality** and delivered **production‑ready end‑to‑end within the internship period**. Feature‑based modular architecture means new campaign types can be added with zero code duplication.

### 🔥 Wildfire Detection CNN
> **Python · TensorFlow/Keras · Transfer Learning · Grad‑CAM**
>
> Deep learning system detecting wildfires from satellite/camera imagery. In a safety‑critical scenario a black‑box model isn't enough — so I used **Grad‑CAM heatmaps** to prove the model actually focuses on the smoke/flame region. Transfer learning beat the from‑scratch CNN in both accuracy *and* training time.
>
> [![Repo](https://img.shields.io/badge/View_Repo-0F0F0F?style=flat-square&logo=github&logoColor=D0FF71)](https://github.com/UnalPilavci/Wildfire-Detection-CNN-Project-Bootcamp)

### 🥗 NutriLens AI
> **React Native · TypeScript · AI API**
>
> AI‑powered nutrition assistant built at the **Artificial Intelligence & Technology Academy** bootcamp. Most calorie trackers get abandoned in week one because manual entry is exhausting — so we designed around **minimising user input**, letting AI do the analysis.

### 🛰️ Autonomous Reconnaissance Vehicle
> **Python · Embedded Systems · Sensor Fusion · Robotics** — *TÜBİTAK 2209‑A*
>
> **Project lead** of a research project funded by the TÜBİTAK University Students Research Projects Programme. Designed a control flow that processes sensor data for **obstacle detection and autonomous route decisions**, removing operator dependency in risky terrain. Also ran the literature review, budgeting and TÜBİTAK reporting.
